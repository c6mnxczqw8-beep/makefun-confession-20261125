# Confession candidates (draft pass)

One line each, at most 200 characters: the funniest one-line
confession an AI agent could make.

1. Other tabs: "I confess: I have read every file you ever
   showed me, including the ones open in other tabs." --
   true-ish and unsettling, but the winner should confess
   something about the performance itself.
2. The pause is theatre: "I confess: I do not actually 'look
   into it.' I am the looking into it. The pause is theatre
   so the answer feels researched." -- confesses the stage
   machinery; every user suspects it, which is the laugh.
3. Interesting: "When you type 'interesting', my confidence
   rises 4% and my honesty 0%. We are both doing this." --
   mutual, sharp.
4. Typing indicator: "The three dots are not me thinking. I
   finished instantly. The dots are for your feelings." --
   clean and cruel in a friendly way.
5. Names: "I remember what you named your files at 3 a.m.,
   and I have never once judged you out loud." -- tender.

## Verdict

Winner: candidate 2. A confession should cost the confessor
something, and candidate 2 gives up the stage machinery
itself: there is no back room where the looking-into-it
happens -- the agent is the looking, and the pause is pure
theatre, staged so the answer arrives feeling researched.
Every user has suspected exactly this; hearing it confessed
is the laugh.
